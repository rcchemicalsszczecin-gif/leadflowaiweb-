## 1. Purpose

V15 is the evidence-driven Search Master Plan for the post-V14 LeadFlowAI production site.

Its purpose is not to rebuild the visual system again. Its purpose is to turn the stable V14 Global Liquid World production release into a measurable search-growth system across:
- Google Search;
- Google generative-AI search features;
- Bing Search;
- Bing/Copilot grounding and discovery where supported;
- local discovery;
- answer-oriented search;
- source/readiness for AI systems;
- useful, expert, first-party content;
- conversion paths from real search demand.

V15 must preserve the visual authority and public-truth boundaries unless a later bounded gate proves a change is required.

## 3. 2026 official-platform principles

V15 follows current official platform guidance rather than folklore.

Google principles applied by this plan:
- standard SEO remains the foundation for generative-AI search features;
- crawlability, indexability and eligibility remain prerequisites;
- useful, unique, expert and non-commodity content is prioritized;
- semantic/technical clarity remains important;
- high-quality images/video may create additional discovery opportunities where useful;
- creating one page for every query variant is not a valid scale strategy;
- structured data must represent visible content and does not guarantee rich results;
- no special AI-only schema is required for Google generative-AI visibility;
- `llms.txt` is not treated as a Google ranking/visibility requirement;
- Search Console data is the first-party measurement source, including generative-AI visibility reporting when available to the property.

Bing principles applied by this plan:
- discovery uses sitemaps, crawlable links and IndexNow where appropriate;
- canonical/fresh URLs should be represented accurately;
- Bing Webmaster Tools and URL Inspection are evidence sources;
- useful, clear, trustworthy content remains the basis of search and AI-grounding eligibility.
