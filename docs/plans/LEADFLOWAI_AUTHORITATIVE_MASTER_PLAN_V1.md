## AEO / GEO / AI Search
AEO/GEO are treated as a search-readiness layer over correct SEO and public truth, not as a separate magic ranking system.

Required principles:
- answer-first structure where it helps users;
- descriptive headings and useful visible FAQs;
- clear entity and relationship language;
- consistent organization/service facts;
- crawlable, indexable and source-friendly pages;
- original, expert, first-party information where real evidence exists;
- useful images/video where they add evidence or explanation;
- no fake citations, rankings, mentions, studies or AI visibility claims;
- no special AI-only markup or files solely for theatre.

For Google generative-AI search features, V15 follows current official guidance that standard SEO, technical accessibility, unique useful content and user value remain the foundation. `llms.txt` is not treated as a Google visibility requirement. If another system demonstrably uses such a file, any implementation must have a separate evidence-backed purpose.

## Structured data
Use Organization, WebSite, WebPage, Service, BreadcrumbList, Article, FAQPage and other types only where semantically valid and consistent with visible content. No schema spam and no invented dates/facts.

Structured data must represent visible page content. Schema is not a substitute for content quality and does not guarantee rich results or AI visibility.
