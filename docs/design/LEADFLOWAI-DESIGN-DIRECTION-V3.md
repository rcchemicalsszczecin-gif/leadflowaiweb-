# LEADFLOWAI DESIGN DIRECTION V3 — CIRCUIT WATER

STATUS: OWNER APPROVED
DATE: 2026-08-13
SUPERSEDES: V2 for background/effect implementation; retains V2 dark editorial hierarchy and reduced typography.

## Core idea
The entire public site behaves like one large living electronic system viewed through a reactive water surface.

The visual environment is not a photographic motherboard wallpaper. It is procedurally rendered so it can continue across every route and through arbitrary page length without seams or repeated image assets.

## Layer model
1. Stable content/UI: headings, body copy, navigation, CTA and forms remain sharp and readable.
2. Translucent technical surfaces: dark glass-like panels expose part of the environment while preserving contrast.
3. Water optics: cursor and click disturbances generate surface height changes, normals, highlights and refraction.
4. Energy/data layer: pulses continuously move along electronic traces and between nodes.
5. Procedural PCB world: traces, chips, pads, pins, grid structure and signal nodes generated by shader math.
6. Obsidian base: dark navy/black environment with restrained cyan and signal-green illumination.

## Water behavior
- Pointer movement is a light brush/muskanie interaction, not a simple cursor glow.
- Click/tap is a stronger stone-impact impulse with multiple delayed wave fronts.
- Water displacement refracts the circuit and energy beneath it rather than drawing unrelated circles over the UI.
- Refraction must not deform critical foreground text or controls.

## Electronic behavior
- PCB traces are always present but low contrast.
- Cyan and signal-green energy pulses travel continuously along selected traces.
- Nodes pulse asynchronously.
- Chip-like structures and pins give macro-scale hardware depth.
- Scroll position changes shader world-space so the user moves across a continuing electronic system.
- Motion must read as energy/data transfer, not random gaming neon.

## Performance constraints
- Native WebGL2; no third-party 3D dependency is required for the baseline V3 implementation.
- Full-screen triangle, one primary fragment pass.
- Device pixel ratio is bounded, with a lower cap on mobile.
- Interactive ripple count is bounded.
- No layout reads per frame other than cached viewport state; scroll updates only a scalar.
- Static fallback exists when WebGL2 is unavailable.

## Accessibility and reduced motion
- The effect is decorative and does not carry information required to use the site.
- Canvas does not capture pointer input.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` stops continuous animation and interactive disturbance and renders a stable frame/fallback environment.
- Foreground contrast and keyboard interaction remain independent of the visual engine.

## Visual constraints
- Entire site remains dark.
- No generic light agency sections.
- No photo wallpaper as the primary background.
- No saturated rainbow/cyberpunk palette.
- Signal green remains an accent; cyan/blue represents circuitry and energy.
- Typography stays smaller than the original first-launch hero while preserving editorial hierarchy.

## Product constraint
The visual effect is a signature demonstration of LeadFlowAI front-end capability, but conversion clarity, SEO/AEO/GEO content, performance and accessibility remain higher priority than spectacle.
