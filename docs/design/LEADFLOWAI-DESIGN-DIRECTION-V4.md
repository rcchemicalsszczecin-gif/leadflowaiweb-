# LEADFLOWAI — DESIGN DIRECTION V4

STATUS: OWNER-APPROVED IMPLEMENTATION CANDIDATE
DATE: 2026-08-13
NAME: Hardware Board V4 / Living Motherboard

## Intent
V4 keeps the approved dark editorial language and refractive water interaction, but makes the electronic environment materially more recognizable and continuously visible.

The background must read as a living motherboard rather than an abstract circuit pattern.

## Hardware language
The procedural WebGL environment must visibly contain motherboard-like structures:
- central CPU package with substrate, die and contact field;
- VRM/power stages around the CPU;
- capacitor-like component banks;
- multiple RAM/DIMM slots;
- PCIe-style expansion slots and lane groups;
- M.2-like storage connector;
- chipset-like secondary processor block;
- dense copper/data traces and edge buses.

These are procedural visual structures, not a copied motherboard image.

## Energy model
Energy/data movement is a primary visual behavior.
- green pulses represent power distribution;
- cyan pulses represent data/interconnect traffic;
- pulses visibly travel between functional hardware regions;
- CPU, VRM, RAM, chipset and expansion lanes participate in the animated system;
- background animation continues while scrolling.

## Water model
The V3 water interaction remains authoritative:
- pointer movement creates light brush-like disturbance;
- click/tap creates a stronger multi-wave stone impact;
- the water normal refracts/deforms the motherboard and energy beneath it;
- foreground text/navigation/CTA remain stable.

## Whole-site visibility
The motherboard is global, not hero-only.
- public content sections use translucent dark surfaces;
- large opaque section bands are prohibited on desktop;
- hardware remains visible between and beneath content blocks through the whole page;
- individual content cards may remain darker/glassier for readability;
- mobile may use stronger section opacity for legibility.

## Performance/accessibility
- native WebGL2 with bounded DPR;
- bounded ripple population;
- static technical fallback when WebGL2 is unavailable;
- `prefers-reduced-motion` stops continuous motion and interactive disturbance;
- no deformation of semantic foreground content.

## Relationship to earlier versions
V4 supersedes V3 where motherboard recognizability and whole-site background visibility conflict.
V3 water physics/refractive interaction remains.
V2 dark editorial hierarchy and restrained typography remain.
