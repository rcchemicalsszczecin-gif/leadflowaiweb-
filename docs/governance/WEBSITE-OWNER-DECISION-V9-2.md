## Decision
V9 remains the design foundation. V9.2 is a calibration layer, not a redesign reset.

The approved direction is:
- widen and safely calibrate the homepage for large desktop viewports;
- eliminate accidental clipping/narrowness;
- reduce unnecessary vertical dead space;
- materially differentiate the six journey scenes;
- strengthen visual depth while retaining the real hardware + water foundation;
- add a dark-void typography manifest;
- make WHAT WE BUILD a major live capability scene;
- add a single browser-native 3D centerpiece without heavyweight dependencies;
- add selective progress/navigation/micro-interaction detail;
- improve stage transitions and motion timing;
- make the final contact a cinematic closing scene;
- preserve reduced-motion/mobile/performance constraints.
