# V15.17 — Original Evidence / Research Gate

STATUS: BLOCKED_NO_AUDITABLE_DATASET
DATE: 2026-08-14
EVIDENCE_CLASS: REPOSITORY_PROOF

## 1. Purpose

Prevent synthetic studies, benchmark theatre and unsupported first-party research from entering public LeadFlowAI content.

Original research is allowed only when a real dataset and reproducible method exist.

## 2. Current state

At this checkpoint no V15 dataset has been admitted as public original research.

Therefore:

`V15_17_ORIGINAL_RESEARCH=BLOCKED_NO_AUDITABLE_DATASET`

No customer or project sample is inferred from portfolio count, route count, CI runs, search observations or internal experiments unless an explicit research dataset is separately defined.

## 3. Mandatory admission package

Before any public study/research claim, record:

- research question;
- dataset source;
- sample definition and inclusion criteria;
- sample size;
- date/time range;
- collection method;
- measurement tools and versions where material;
- exclusions and missing-data handling;
- calculations or transformation steps;
- reproducible query/script/calculation path where safe;
- limitations;
- privacy/data-use authority;
- exact release/source revision.

## 4. Acceptable future first-party study classes

Potential research only when real data exists:

- controlled performance experiments on first-party LeadFlowAI/Tervyxa properties;
- static-site crawl/schema experiments with recorded inputs and outputs;
- documented AI Search source observations with fixed query/sample methodology;
- accessibility/performance engineering comparisons;
- anonymized operational studies only with appropriate authority and sufficient sample quality.

## 5. Forbidden

- fake sample sizes;
- invented percentages;
- synthetic customer KPI presented as observed business data;
- extrapolating three first-party portfolio projects into a market study;
- presenting CI test volume as user behavior;
- generated survey responses;
- fabricated Search Console/Bing data;
- claiming causal ranking impact from uncontrolled before/after observations;
- customer data publication without authority.

## 6. Publication rule

A research artifact must remain internal/non-claiming until the admission package is complete. If the dataset cannot support a conclusion, report the limitation rather than strengthening the claim.

## 7. Verdict

`V15_17_ORIGINAL_RESEARCH=BLOCKED_NO_AUDITABLE_DATASET`

`PUBLIC_STUDY_CLAIMS=NONE`

`FABRICATED_RESEARCH=FORBIDDEN`

`RESUME_REQUIRES=REAL_DATASET+METHOD+LIMITATIONS+AUTHORITY`
